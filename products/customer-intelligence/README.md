# Customer Intelligence — Churn Prediction, RFM Segmentation & CLV Backtesting

> Scores telecom subscribers and retail cohorts for churn risk and 12-month revenue at risk — reaching **PR-AUC 0.841** (95% CI `0.806–0.870`), **ROC-AUC 0.933**, and **81.2% call precision at a 20% contact budget** (`$60,353` annualised revenue at risk across the top 50 accounts), paired with an analytical **BG/NBD-style CLV backtest within +0.34% of actual 6-month holdout revenue**.
