Here are the exact numbers from 3 executive Power BI workbooks I built across Telecom Churn (7,043 users), Contact Center Ops (5,000 calls), and HR Inclusion (500 staff). 🧵👇

2/6 Case Study 1 — Telecom MRR Churn (7,043 subscribers):
• Overall churn: 26.54% (1,869 churned users)
• MRR lost to churn: $139,130.85/mo (30.50% of $456,116.60 total MRR)
• Month-to-month churn is 42.71% vs 11.27% (1-yr) and 2.83% (2-yr) — a 15.1x risk gap.

3/6 Why are high-ARPU Fiber Optic users churning at 41.89% (vs 18.96% on DSL)?
Look at support tickets: churned users logged 1.163 technical tickets on average vs 0.151 for retained users (7.69x higher). Electronic check payers also churn at 45.29% vs 15.24% on auto-credit card.

4/6 Case Study 2 — Inbound Contact Center SLA (5,000 calls):
• Answered rate: 81.08% (18.92% abandoned in queue; mean answer speed 67.5s)
• First-contact resolution once answered: 89.94% (72.92% of total inbound)
• Technical Support has the highest abandonment rate across all 5 queues at 21.00%.

5/6 Case Study 3 — Workforce Diversity & Promotion Equity (500 employees):
• Base representation: 59.0% Male (295) / 41.0% Female (205)
• FY21 promotion velocity: 11.19% Male vs 8.78% Female (1.27x gap)
